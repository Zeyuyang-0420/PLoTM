# Citing PLoTM and its components

PLoTM is a pipeline that integrates and reproduces several published methods and datasets. If you
use it, please cite the underlying works:

**Method (regulators → programs → traits):**
Ota M, et al. *Causal modelling of gene effects from regulators to programs to traits.* Nature (2026).
doi:10.1038/s41586-025-09866-3

**Consensus NMF (cNMF):**
Kotliar D, et al. *Identifying gene expression programs of cell-type identity and cellular activity
with single-cell RNA-Seq.* eLife (2019).

**GeneBayes / gene-level LoF posterior:**
Zeng T, Spence JP, et al. GeneBayes (shet / gene-level Bayesian estimation).

**LoF genetics — two sources:**
Karczewski KJ, et al. *Systematic single-variant and gene-based association testing of 3,700
phenotypes in 394,841 UK Biobank exomes* (GeneBass). Cell Genomics (2022).
Backman JD, et al. *Exome sequencing and analysis of 454,787 UK Biobank participants.* Nature (2021).
(Burden summary statistics obtained via the GWAS Catalog.)

**Perturb-seq atlas (reference configuration):**
Zhu R, Dann E, Yan J, et al. Genome-scale CRISPRi Perturb-seq in primary human CD4⁺ T cells
(Pritchard / Marson labs, via CZI virtualcellmodels). Preprint 2025.
Replogle JM, et al. (K562 genome-wide Perturb-seq — the paper's original substrate). Cell (2022).
